# Second pass — pick a direction

**Nothing in `mobile/` has changed.** Open the PNGs, or the HTML in a browser
for the crisp version.

## What was wrong with the first attempt

- **Dark base with purple.** Dropped entirely. Both were mine to get wrong.
- **Two themes.** Dropped. One interface.
- **The header and the active tab were both violet**, adjacent, so they bled
  into each other. The tab strip now sits on the page below the bar with a
  clear gap, and its selected state is a white pill — it never repeats the
  bar's colour.
- **Summary was bolted onto Tasks.** It is its own landing page now:
  **Summary · Tasks · Payments**, three siblings.

## The three directions

All use the same palette. All are light. None of them puts purple behind the
logo.

| | Base | Bar | Character |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A · Paper** | warm off-white `#F7F5F1` | charcoal `#17181C` | Quiet. A true neutral bar with no violet in it; colour appears only where it means something. |
| **B · Mint** | cool paper `#F2FAF8` | deep teal `#0A2E2C` | The yellow mark is at its strongest against teal — complementary colours, so the logo sings rather than sits. Mint carries money throughout. |
| **C · Blocks** | white | near-black `#16161C` | Loudest. The palette as large fills rather than accents. |

## What every direction keeps

The palette's five colours, each with one job:

```
#9B5DE5  purple   primary action        (A and C)
#00F5D4  mint     money — paid, earned  (B uses it as the brand too)
#00BBF9  blue     information — review queue
#F15BB5  pink     attention — overdue, sent back, registrations
#FEE440  yellow   heat on the ladder
```

Text-weight versions of each are darkened until they pass contrast on a light
surface; the raw neons stay for fills and bars.

**The mark is untouched and nothing else uses its yellow at full strength**,
so it is always the brightest thing on screen.

## My read

**B · Mint.** Teal is yellow's complement, so the mark is at its strongest
there, and it is the direction furthest from what was rejected. A is the safe
one and will not offend anybody. C is the most striking and the most likely
to tire.

Tell me which, and anything to change within it, and I will build it.
