# Privacy Policy — Kahiniscope Production

**Last updated: 14 September 2026**

Kahiniscope Production is an internal production-management app for the
Kahiniscope Bengali audio-drama channel. It is used by the channel's admin and
its contributors to assign and track episode production tasks.

This policy describes exactly what the app collects, why, and who it reaches.

## Who runs this app

Kahiniscope. Contact: **owner@kahiniscope.example**.

## What is collected, and why

The app collects only what it needs to assign work and to remind you about it.

| What | Why | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your name and email address | To identify you to the admin, and to sign you in | Your Google account, when you sign in |
| Your phone number | To send reminders by WhatsApp or SMS if those channels are enabled | You type it during registration |
| Your craft (Voice, Editing, and so on) | So the admin knows what to assign you | You choose it during registration |
| A note you write | So you can tell the admin anything useful about yourself | You type it during registration |
| Your Telegram chat id | To send reminders to your Telegram, if you connect it | Created when you press Start on the bot |
| Your device's notification token | To send push notifications to your phone | Your device, if you allow notifications |
| Your tasks, and when you completed them | The purpose of the app | Assigned by the admin; completed by you |
| A log of reminders sent to you | So the admin can check whether a reminder was actually delivered | Created by the app when it sends |

**The app does not collect** your location, your contacts, your photos, your
files, anything from other apps, or any advertising identifier. It contains no
advertising and no analytics.

## Who can see it

- **You** can see your own record and your own tasks, and nothing else.
- **The channel's admins** can see every member's name, email, phone number,
  craft, note, tasks and reminder history. That is the point of the app.
- **Nobody else.** The data is not sold, rented, or shared with any third
  party for their own purposes.

## Where it is stored

In Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), in Google's
`asia-south1` region in Mumbai, India. Google processes it on our behalf as our
hosting provider.

## Services that receive your data in order to deliver a reminder

A reminder is sent through the first of these that can reach you. Only the
information needed to deliver that one message is sent:

- **Firebase Cloud Messaging** (Google) — your device notification token and
  the reminder text.
- **Telegram** — your Telegram chat id and the reminder text, only if you have
  connected Telegram.
- **WhatsApp Cloud API** (Meta) — your phone number and the reminder text, only
  if the admin has enabled WhatsApp.
- **Textbelt** — your phone number and the reminder text, only if the admin has
  enabled SMS.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy and handles your data under
its own terms.

## How long it is kept

Your record and your task history are kept for as long as you are part of the
team. Reminder logs are kept so the admin can check delivery.

## Your choices

- **Notifications** can be refused or turned off in your phone's settings. You
  will still be reminded on the other channels.
- **Telegram** is optional. If you never connect it, nothing is sent there.
- **Deletion.** Ask the admin at owner@kahiniscope.example to remove your account.
  Declining or deleting an account removes your user record and your sign-in
  from the system. You can also revoke the app's access to your Google account
  at myaccount.google.com.

## Children

The app is for the channel's production team. It is not directed at children
and is not intended for anyone under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
