## Full description (4000 characters max)

```
Kahiniscope Production is the internal task board for the Kahiniscope Bengali
audio-drama channel — গল্পপাঠ, episode after episode.

One admin assigns the work that makes an episode: script writing, translation,
voice recording, dubbing and mixing, editing, thumbnails and graphics, upload
and SEO, music and SFX, proofreading. Everyone else sees their own tasks and
one button to close them.

WHAT IT DOES

• Assign a task to a person, for an episode, with a due date.
• Watch an episode fill up — completion is shown for the whole slate, for each
  episode, and for each member inside an episode.
• Chase automatically. A task nobody closes is reminded after 7 days, then
  after 4, 3, 2 and 1 — and then every day until it is done. Marking it done
  stops the reminders immediately.
• Reminders arrive as a phone notification, or on Telegram, where a member can
  close the task straight from the chat without opening the app.
• See what was actually sent, on which channel, and when.

WHO IT IS FOR

The Kahiniscope production team. Anyone can install it and register, but new
sign-ups see a holding screen until the channel's admin approves them, and can
see nothing else until then.

HOW ACCESS WORKS

Sign in with Google. Your registration goes to the admin, who approves you and
sets what you do. Admins can approve people, assign work and send reminders.
One account — the channel's own — can promote admins and change how hard the
app chases.

The interface is in English. Episode titles are in Bengali.
```
