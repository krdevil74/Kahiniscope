# Deleting your Kahiniscope Production account

This page is for **Kahiniscope Production** (`com.kahiniscope.production`),
the internal task app for the Kahiniscope Bengali audio-drama channel.

It explains how to have your account and your data removed, what is deleted,
and what is kept.

## How to ask

Send an email to <support address> from **the same address you sign in
with**, with the subject `Delete my account`.

That is the whole procedure. There is nothing to fill in and no form to find.
Asking from the address you sign in with is what lets the admin match the
request to the right account without asking you for anything else.

You will get a reply confirming it is done. Allow up to 30 days, though in
practice it is the same week.

You can also remove the app's access to your Google account at any time at
[myaccount.google.com](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). That stops
the app being able to sign you in, but it does not by itself delete what is
already stored — send the email for that.

## What is deleted

- Your **user record**: your name, your email address, your phone number, the
  crafts you work in, and the note you wrote when you registered.
- Your **sign-in**, so the account can no longer be used to open the app.
- Your **Telegram connection**, if you ever made one.
- Your **notification tokens**, so nothing further can be sent to your phone.

## What is kept, and for how long

- **The work itself.** A task that was assigned and completed stays on the
  episode it belongs to, because it is a record of what was made rather than
  information about you. It carries no name once your user record is gone.
- **Payment records** for work already paid for are kept while they are needed
  as an account of what was paid, and no longer than one year.

Everything on this app is deleted automatically after **one year** regardless
of any request: episodes, tasks and the record of reminders sent are swept
weekly once they are older than that. Nothing is kept indefinitely.
