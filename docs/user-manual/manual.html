Kahiniscope
Member Guide

How to join the team, receive and hand in your work, and get paid — step by step, with screenshots from the app.

1 · JoinCreate an account and send your registration for approval.
2 · WorkSee your tasks, open the script, do the work.
3 · Hand inSend the file, then tap Submit for review.
4 · Get paidApproved work becomes a payment. Check it and keep the screenshot.

The screenshots in this guide use sample names, episodes and amounts.

STEP 1

Install the app and create your account

Install Kahiniscope Production from the Google Play Store and open it. There are two ways in:

A. With your Google account

Tap Continue with Google and pick the Google account you use for the channel.

B. With an email and password

  1. Tap New here? Create an account.
  2. Type your email address and choose a password of at least 8 characters.
  3. Tap Create account.

Next time, type the same email and password and tap Sign in with email. Forgot it? Type your email and tap Forgot password? — a reset link is emailed to you.

Use only one of the two ways, and the same one each time. If you joined with Google, keep using Continue with Google.
STEP 2

Fill in your registration and send it for approval

The first time you sign in, the app asks who you are. The admin uses this to decide whether to approve you and what work to give you.

  1. Your name — the name the team knows you by. This is what the admin sees when assigning work.
  2. What you do — tap every kind of work you do (up to 5): Script, Translation, Voice, Post / mix, Graphics, Proofreading, Editing.
  3. Phone — your WhatsApp number. Reminders can reach you here.
  4. Anything the admin should know — earlier episodes you worked on, when you are free, anything useful.
  5. Tap Send for approval.
STEP 3

Wait for the admin to approve you

Your registration now sits in the admin's Requests list. Until it is approved you will see this screen — there is nothing else to do.

  • The screen moves on by itself the moment you are approved. You can also pull down to check.
  • You will get a welcome message saying you are approved.
Example messageHi Maya, you are approved as Translation · Voice on Kahiniscope. Open the app to see anything assigned to you — reminders will arrive here too.

Approval is done by hand by the admin, so it may take a little while.

STEP 4

Your home screen

Once approved, you land on Summary. The three tabs at the top are all you need:

TabWhat it shows
SummaryHow many tasks are pending, submitted and approved; what you have earned; what is still to be paid (Pending est.).
TasksThe work assigned to you, with deadlines and the script.
PaymentsEverything you have been paid, and what is waiting to be paid.

When the admin assigns you a task, you get a notification and it appears under Tasks.

STEP 5

Open your task and the script

Go to Tasks. Each card is one piece of work:

  • Episode code and title, and the type of work (e.g. Voice recording).
  • Submission deadline — and how late it is, if it has passed.
  • A label for how urgent it is: On track, or Escalated when reminders have been going for a while.

Get the script

Tap Open the script on the task card. The script opens in Google Drive, where you can read or download it. It is there only for episodes you have a task on.

Now do the work.

A task card with its script link, a note typed in, and the Submit for review button.

STEP 6

Hand in your work

Handing in is two parts: send the file, then tell the app.

  1. Send the finished file to the admin on WhatsApp or Telegram, the way your team agreed.
  2. In the app, on that task's card, type a short note in the box — where you sent the file, or what you changed.
  3. Tap Submit for review.

The card changes to Waiting for review. There is nothing more to do; the admin checks it next.

From Telegram: if Telegram is connected (Step 7), each reminder has a ✓ Submit for review button. Tapping it does the same as the button in the app — send the file first.

After submitting: the task is in review.

Always tap Submit for review — in the app, or on a Telegram reminder. Sending the file on WhatsApp or Telegram alone does not tell the app you are done, so the reminders will continue.
STEP 7

Reminders — until you submit

While a task is open, Kahiniscope keeps reminding you. Reminders stop the moment you tap Submit for review.

  • The first reminder comes about a week after the task is assigned. After that they come more and more often — a few days apart, then every day — until you submit.
  • They arrive as an app notification first, then on Telegram (and on WhatsApp, if your team has switched it on) — whichever reaches you.
  • No reminders are sent at night (quiet hours); those wait until the morning.
Example reminderVoice recording · EP-41 · 4 days late — … Submit it in the app when it is ready.

Get reminders on Telegram (recommended)

At the bottom of Tasks, tap Connect Telegram. Telegram opens; press Start in the Kahiniscope bot chat. You will get a message saying your chat is linked.

The Connect Telegram card, and the note explaining how reminders are sent. Once connected, each Telegram reminder carries a ✓ Submit for review button.

STEP 8

The admin reviews your work

Every submission becomes a review request for the admin. They either approve it or send it back. Either way, you are notified.

If it is sent back (rejected)

The task returns to your list with the admin's feedback in a dark box, starting Sent back:. Read it, redo the work, send the new file, and tap Submit again.

A task sent back with feedback. Reminders resume (every other day) until it is submitted again.

Example messageHi Rizu, your Voice recording has come back for changes: Room echo in scene 3 — please re-record lines 12 to 18 and send again. Submit it again in the app when it is ready.

If it is approved

The task is accepted and a payment is created for it automatically. You get a message with the estimated amount:

Example messageHi Rizu, your Voice recording has been approved. ₹840 is now pending — the final amount is set by the admin when they pay.
STEP 9

Your payment — estimated, then paid

Open Payments.

  • Paid tasks — money you have received, with the total at the top.
  • Upcoming payments — approved work not yet paid, marked Waiting to be paid with an estimate.

The estimate is your rate × the amount of work (for example ₹60 per minute × 14 minutes). The admin confirms the final amount when paying, so it can differ slightly.

Approved work waiting to be paid, with its estimated amount.

STEP 10

Payment done — check it and keep the screenshot

When the admin pays you, you get a notification, and the payment moves to Paid tasks with the real amount.

Example messageHi Rizu, ₹720 has been paid to you for Voice recording.

If the admin attached the payment screenshot (bank / UPI confirmation), a ⤓ download button appears beside the amount. Tap how? to see how the amount was worked out and how long the screenshot is kept.

A paid task with its screenshot: tap ⤓ to save it to your phone's photos. "20 days left" shows how long it stays available.

Screenshots are kept for 30 days

Each payment screenshot is deleted automatically 30 days after it is added. Save it with ⤓ before then if you want to keep it. After that, the payment itself stays — amount, date and calculation — but the screenshot is gone and the row says Screenshot expired.

An older payment: details still there, screenshot expired.

Something not working?

  • Stuck on "Waiting for approval" — the admin has not approved you yet. Pull down to refresh, or message the admin.
  • "That email and password do not match" — check the spelling, or use Forgot password?. If you joined with Google, use Continue with Google.
  • No notifications — allow notifications for Kahiniscope in your phone's settings, and connect Telegram as a backup.
  • No ⤓ on a paid task — either no screenshot was attached, or it is older than 30 days.
